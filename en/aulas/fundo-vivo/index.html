Lesson 6 · 18 min

Live background: video generated inside the site

A long opening film comes from several short clips chained together, each starting on the last real frame of the one before. The risk is a hidden cut in the middle of a clip. The site-movimento skill has gates that catch it. You run them on rehearsal clips, one continuous and one with a cut, without spending credits.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The seven steps of the site-movimento skill and where the gates come in
  2. 2The engine contract (Kling, Agnes, Kie, your own) tested without spending, with DRY_RUN
  3. 3Assemble the film and prove continuity; catch the hidden cut
  4. 4The copy gate in Portuguese, English, and Spanish

Goal: chain clips into a continuous film and prove with the site-movimento gates that there is no hidden cut.

01 · Chained clips, no jump

Without a video engine, the skill makes the film in code (steps 1, 2, 5, 6, and 7 are the same). Pilot numbers: 300 Kling 3.0 credits at 720p, 285 frames in the horizontal film and 361 in the vertical one (README, 10/18/2026).

02 · The engine contract, without spending

With DRY_RUN=1 each engine only shows what it would do. The test checks all four.

Terminal · download site-movimento and test the engines
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-sites/a6; rm -rf "$E"; mkdir -p "$E"; cd "$E"
git clone -q https://github.com/inematds/site-movimento && git -C site-movimento checkout -q d9a43a4; echo "clone: $?"
bash site-movimento/skill/site-movimento/tests/motores-contrato.sh; echo "saída: $?"
Example from the repository

OK kling
OK agnes
OK kie
OK custom
4/4 motores OK
saída: 0

No service was called: DRY_RUN exits before reading any key. Actually generating uses the account and credits of the engine you choose; the course doesn't do that.

03 · Two chained clips become the site's film

The rehearsal imitates the chaining: clip 2 starts at the zoom where clip 1 ended.

Terminal · chain, assemble, and prove continuity
K=~/projetos/formacao-cinema-ia-sites-v72/kit
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-sites/a6; SM=$E/site-movimento/skill/site-movimento/scripts
# dois clipes encadeados: o 2º começa exatamente onde o 1º terminou (zoom 1,25)
bash "$K/scripts/clipe.sh" "$K/exemplos/lente.jpg" "$E/c1.mp4" 4 1.0 1.25
bash "$K/scripts/clipe.sh" "$K/exemplos/lente.jpg" "$E/c2.mp4" 4 1.25 1.5
python3 "$SM/cortes.py" "$E/c1.mp4" "$E/c2.mp4" | sed "s#$E/##"; echo "cortes: ${PIPESTATUS[0]}"
rm -rf "$E/site"; cp -r "$K/site" "$E/site"
python3 "$SM/montar.py" "$E/site" "$E/c1.mp4:0:-1" "$E/c2.mp4:1:-1" | head -1 | sed "s#$E/##"
python3 "$SM/continuidade.py" "$E/site/frames" | sed "s#$E/##"; echo "saída: ${PIPESTATUS[0]}"
Example from the repository
c1.mp4: 4,0 s · 24 fps · 1920×1080
c2.mp4: 4,0 s · 24 fps · 1920×1080
c1.mp4 quadros 97, mediana 1.00, piores [(77, 0.99), (90, 0.99)] ok
c2.mp4 quadros 97, mediana 1.00, piores [(1, 0.99), (95, 0.99)] ok
cortes: 0
site: 191 quadros, 3.4 MB, cor da emenda #050605
continuidade — site/frames: 48 quadros (a cada 4), mediana 0.983
PASSA — um movimento só
saída: 0

4 s + 4 s = 191 frames (frame 0 of clip 2 is dropped so it isn't repeated). montar.py writes to site/frames, the folder the kit page already reads.

04 · The hidden cut

A clip that jumps scenes in the middle: the lens in the dark studio and, suddenly, on the light background.

Terminal · a clip with a cut
K=~/projetos/formacao-cinema-ia-sites-v72/kit
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-sites/a6; SM=$E/site-movimento/skill/site-movimento/scripts
# a clip with a hidden cut in the middle: the lens in the dark studio and, suddenly, on the light background
bash "$K/scripts/clipe.sh" "$K/exemplos/lente.jpg" "$E/a.mp4" 2 1.0 1.1
bash "$K/scripts/clipe.sh" "$K/exemplos/lente-clara.jpg" "$E/b.mp4" 2 1.1 1.2
ffmpeg -v error -y -i "$E/a.mp4" -i "$E/b.mp4" -filter_complex "[0:v][1:v]concat=n=2:v=1[v]" -map "[v]" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 "$E/c3.mp4"
python3 "$SM/cortes.py" "$E/c3.mp4" | sed "s#$E/##"; echo "saída: ${PIPESTATUS[0]}"
Example from the repository
a.mp4: 2,0 s · 24 fps · 1920×1080
b.mp4: 2,0 s · 24 fps · 1920×1080
c3.mp4 quadros 97, mediana 1.00, piores [(48, 0.46), (72, 0.99)] CORTE no(s) quadro(s) [48]
saída: 1

The frame pair at 48 drops to 0.46 against a median of 1.00: a cut. A cut between two dark, similar frames can slip past cortes.py; that's why the skill also runs continuidade.py and you watch the film.

05 · The clip queue

Paid clips go out in order, one at a time. Run it, interrupt it, and run it again: only what's missing comes back.
