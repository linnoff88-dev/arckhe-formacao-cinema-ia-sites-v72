Lesson 2 · 15 min

From video to frames: extract, compress, and order the sequence

The quality of the scroll motion is decided here. Too few frames and the film turns into a slideshow; frames that are too big and the page won't open on a phone; names without zeros and the film jumps. You make a rehearsal clip with ffmpeg from a photo, extract every frame, and check count, order, and weight with a script.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1A rehearsal clip from a photo, no generator
  2. 2Extract every frame, at the clip's rate, with the width and quality you chose
  3. 3Why f_0001.jpg and not f_1.jpg, and what manifest.json does
  4. 4Check count, order, and weight before taking it to the page

Goal: turn a clip into frames at the native rate, with names in order and weight checked, ready for scroll.

Rule from ARCKHÉ's two skills: extract at the native rate. A 72 s film at 24 frames per second has 1,728 frames; taking "about 300" becomes 4 frames per second on scroll (scroll-film-studio, references/engine.md).

03 · Extract and check

quadros.sh extracts; conferir-quadros.py compares the folder with the clip.

Terminal · extract the frames and check
K=~/projetos/formacao-cARCKHÉ-ia-sites-v72/kit
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-sites/a2
bash "$K/scripts/quadros.sh" "$E/clipe.mp4" "$E/quadros" 1024 6
ls "$E/quadros" | head -3
python3 "$K/scripts/conferir-quadros.py" "$E/quadros" "$E/clipe.mp4"; echo "saída: $?"
File · kit/scripts/quadros.sh
#!/usr/bin/env bash
# quadros.sh — extrai TODOS os quadros de um clipe, na taxa nativa dele, para o site rolar.
# Uso: bash quadros.sh <clipe.mp4> <pasta> [largura=1024] [qualidade=6]
#   largura: 1024–1280 px no computador, 720 px no filme vertical do celular.
#   qualidade: -q:v do JPEG (2 = melhor e mais pesado, 31 = pior); 5–6 é o ponto do curso.
# Grava <pasta>/f_0001.jpg, f_0002.jpg… (zeros à esquerda: a ordem alfabética é a ordem do filme)
# e <pasta>/manifest.json {count, fps, largura}, que a página lê para saber quantos quadros pedir.
set -euo pipefail
CLIPE=$1; PASTA=$2; LARG=${3:-1024}; Q=${4:-6}
FPS=$(ffprobe -v error -select_streams v:0 -show_entries stream=r_frame_rate -of csv=p=0 "$CLIPE" | python3 -c "import sys; n,d=sys.stdin.read().strip().split('/'); print(round(int(n)/int(d)))")
mkdir -p "$PASTA"; rm -f "$PASTA"/f_*.jpg
# -fps_mode passthrough: um JPEG por quadro do clipe, sem duplicar nem pular.
ffmpeg -v error -i "$CLIPE" -vf "scale=${LARG}:-2" -fps_mode passthrough -q:v "$Q" "$PASTA/f_%04d.jpg"
python3 - "$PASTA" "$FPS" "$LARG" <<'PY'
import glob, json, os, sys
from PIL import Image
pasta, fps, larg = sys.argv[1], int(sys.argv[2]), int(sys.argv[3])
q = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(pasta, 'f_*.jpg')))
json.dump({'count': len(q), 'fps': fps, 'largura': larg}, open(os.path.join(pasta, 'manifest.json'), 'w'))
w, h = Image.open(q[0]).size
mb = f"{sum(os.path.getsize(f) for f in q) / 1e6:.1f}".replace('.', ',')
print(f"QUADROS: {len(q)} · {w}×{h} · {mb} MB · {fps} fps")
PY
Example from the repository
QUADROS: 144 · 1024×576 · 1,5 MB · 24 fps
f_0001.jpg
f_0002.jpg
f_0003.jpg
OK: 144 quadros · 24 fps · 1024 px · 1,5 MB
saída: 0

144 frames = 6 s × 24. The folder weighs 1.5 MB at 1024 px with -q:v 6.

04 · The two mistakes that ruin the film the most

Too few frames and names without zeros. Both go unnoticed when you just look at the folder.

Terminal · extract wrong on purpose
K=~/projetos/formacao-cARCKHÉ-ia-sites-v72/kit
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-sites/a2; mkdir -p "$E/poucos" "$E/sem-zero"
# mistake 1: "a few frames per second is enough" (6 per second from a 24 fps clip)
ffmpeg -v error -i "$E/clipe.mp4" -vf "fps=6,scale=1024:-2" -q:v 6 "$E/poucos/f_%04d.jpg"
python3 "$K/scripts/conferir-quadros.py" "$E/poucos" "$E/clipe.mp4"; echo "saída: $?"
# mistake 2: names without leading zeros (f_%d instead of f_%04d)
ffmpeg -v error -i "$E/clipe.mp4" -vf "scale=1024:-2" -q:v 6 "$E/sem-zero/f_%d.jpg"
python3 "$K/scripts/conferir-quadros.py" "$E/sem-zero" "$E/clipe.mp4"; echo "saída: $?"
Example from the repository
FALHA: 36 quadros = 6 fps (fonte 24): esperava 144
FALHA: sem manifest.json
saída: 1
FALHA: nomes sem zeros à esquerda (f_109.jpg vem antes de f_11.jpg na ordem alfabética)
FALHA: sem manifest.json
saída: 1

Both exit with code 1. The checker also demands the manifest.json, which only quadros.sh writes.
