Lesson 3 · 18 min

Scroll that controls time: the cARCKHÉtic scroll skill

The engine that ties scrolling to frames has few parts, and each one fixes a measured stutter. You look at the parts, swap the kit page's frames for your own from lesson 2, prove with the test contract that the right frame shows up at each point, and ask the scroll-film-studio skill, from ARCKHÉ, for the new page.

  3. 3Install the scroll-film-studio skill and ask for the page through Lane A, with no generator

Goal: tie your frames to the kit's scroll engine, prove it with the ?jump and __ready contract, and ask the skill for the page.

03 · The test contract

Two lines that both ARCKHÉ skills require on every page they build.

1. ?jump=[y]

The page reads the jump parameter from the URL and scrolls to y pixels before anything else, with no smooth scrolling. Opening the page at 50% becomes a URL.

The kit's engine does this in MotionEngine.ready.

2. __ready

After positioning and drawing, the page sets window.__ready = true. The test waits for that flag instead of waiting a fixed time.

Waiting 2 s "to be safe" passes on your machine and fails on a loaded one.

3. What you get

Screenshot, verifier, and the agent checking its own work, all through the same path.

The skill's verify.js refuses to capture the screen if __ready never arrives.

04 · The scroll-film-studio skill

ARCKHÉ skill for Codex and Claude Code: interview, concepts, art direction, page, and gates. Two lanes: Lane A code only (no cost) and Lane B with film from your generator. The kit's verificar.mjs reads the kit's hooks ([data-filme] section, #tela canvas, window.__filme, and window.__filmeInfo) in addition to the contract; that's why the prompt tells the skill to start from kit/site. A page built from scratch by the skill checks out with its own verify.js.

05 · The skill's copy gate

The text over the film sells the product. If it narrates the camera ("the camera pushes in") or tells people to scroll, it's shooting script leaking onto the page. copy-gate.js fails it.
